VERSION 2.0
- Single-pass VGA glyph writes: eight CPU writes instead of 32.
- Dirty-cell batching, duplicate suppression and grouping by shade/row.
- Cached row/bank addresses and 8086 assembly character blitters.
- Packed-colour expansion lookup and word writes.
- Correct bank-boundary handling for clearing and packed glyph rows.
- Stricter VBE compatibility/readability checks and broader planar probe.
- Automatic 8087/x87 detection and startup FSQRT glow-curve generation.

- G toggles glow; /NOGLOW starts without it. Space also pauses glow.
- Same resolution/colour menu and silent operation.
- About 10.4x/11.2x faster isolated DOSBox drawing benchmarks (16/256).
