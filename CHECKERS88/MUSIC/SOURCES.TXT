3D CHECKERS 88 - MUSIC SOURCES AND LICENCES

These four pieces were converted from the Mutopia Project's MIDI files.
The original MIDI and LilyPond files are included beside this document.
No recording or sampled instrument is included. The player uses an original
OPL2 piano-style patch with up to eight music voices and a separate FX voice.

CHANGES FOR THE GAME
Converted by ChatGPT (GPT-6 Astra / Codex) for this project, 2026-10-15.
The MIDI note times are rounded to the 36.4 Hz PIT clock. All note attacks
are retained, including both hands and harmony; maximum simultaneous voices
in these sources is six. MIDI velocity becomes FM carrier attenuation.
Sustain-pedal commands are replaced by the FM envelope's release, and the
piano/hymn sound is replaced by the game's original FM piano instrument.
A two-second breath is added after each piece before the next track.
No musical endorsement by the composers, editors or Mutopia is implied.

Approximate durations before the two-second gap:
  Blue Danube theme 39 seconds; Fur Elise 2:10; Moonlight I 4:36;
  Ode to Joy 38 seconds. The playlist loops, or advances in shuffled order.

REBUILD
  python3 MUSIC/CONVERT.PY
This regenerates MUSIC.H with the Python standard library only. Checks in
the converter reject an out-of-range note, voice stealing or excess events
per tick. No MIDI player, external music file or tracker is needed in DOS.
