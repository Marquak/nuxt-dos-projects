LIMITS
------
Physical NEC timing calibration, NuXT USE!UMBS/DOSMAX totals and empty-8087
socket behavior still need bare-metal testing. QEMU is not a V20/8087 timing
model. No 286/386/8088 physical tests, alternate UMB managers or DOS 2 tests
were performed. Disk failures and UMB-link restoration failure are handled
but were not forced in the guest. CPU samples assume normal BIOS timer rate.
